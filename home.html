🙋 Questions, answered straight

Everything people ask before the quiz

What exactly do I get for the $1?＋
Your finished one-product store — built by the AI around the product you chose — unlocked and prepared for launch on Shopify, plus 3 days of full access to everything in the membership. You'll have already seen the complete store preview before the $1 is ever charged.
Then it's $49/month? How does billing work?＋
Yes — plainly: $1 today, then $49/month starting on day 4, billed automatically until you cancel. The subscription covers your store's software, supplier connection, and support. We show this at checkout before you pay, it's in the Purchase Agreement, and we email you a receipt for every charge.
How do I cancel?＋
Two ways, both easy: use the cancellation link in any of our emails / your billing portal, or email support@sellproducts.ai with the subject "Cancel." Cancellation stops all future charges immediately. No phone calls, no retention mazes.
Do I need experience — or my own product ideas?＋
No. That's the point of the quiz: the AI matches products to your goals, experience level, and available time. Most of our customers are building their first store. You pick from finished product cards — supplier verified, prices set, margin shown.
Who ships the products?＋
Your store's connected supplier. When a customer orders, the supplier packs and ships directly to them with tracking. You never buy inventory upfront and never handle a box — you pay the supplier's cost per order only after a customer has paid you.
Are there other costs I should know about?＋
Honest answer: yes, running a store has costs beyond us. Shopify bills its own hosting subscription (they set their pricing and promos — often $1/month for the first 3 months for new stores). You'll also pay per-order supplier costs, and any advertising you choose to run. We spell all of this out in the Purchase Agreement. Also: if you sign up for Shopify through our link, we may earn an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you.
