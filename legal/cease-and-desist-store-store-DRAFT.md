## Before sending — checklist

- [ ] Identify the operator's legal entity (site footer, terms/privacy pages, a test purchase receipt, WHOIS, Meta Page Transparency). Letters addressed to a real entity get taken seriously; letters addressed to a domain often do not.
- [ ] File the copyright application (special handling if litigation is likely) so the letter can cite a registration or application number.
- [ ] File the USPTO trademark application for the word mark and the logo.
- [ ] Have the attorney confirm the 72-hour deadline, governing law and venue language for your state.
- [ ] Send the DMCA notices and the Meta IP report in parallel; do not wait for the letter's deadline.
